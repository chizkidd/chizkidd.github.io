Projects

Applications & Tools

  • nbview - Browser-based Jupyter notebook viewer and diff tool for GitHub repositories, gists, and direct URLs. No uploads or backend required.
  • Inkcast - Client-side, multi-voice audiobook player for EPUB, PDF, and HTML, with no account or backend. Your files stay on your device.
  • Paper-Trail - Evidence-grounded document Q&A combining hybrid lexical and semantic retrieval, cross-encoder reranking, and answer verification.
  • AlphaRadar - Financial research engine for screening and ranking potentially undervalued companies with asymmetric upside.

Interactive Explorations

  • Inference Playground - Explore continuous batching, paged KV caching, and the decode roofline, with an engine-vs-vLLM benchmark comparison using Tesla T4 results from minivLLM.
  • FlashAttention Tiling - Visualize tiled attention computation and online softmax, showing how FlashAttention avoids materializing the full attention matrix.
  • Igbo Tone-Loss Explorer - Explore how speech recognition handles Igbo tone diacritics across 21 annotated recordings, including clip-level error analysis and bootstrap uncertainty intervals.
  • TD(λ) and the Deadly Triad - Explore eligibility traces, λ parameter sweeps on the 19-state random walk, and Baird's counterexample with and without TDC.
  • NBA MVP Ballot Box - Explore the top five MVP vote-getters across every NBA season from 1955-56 to 2025-26, with career heat maps, head-to-head comparisons, historical records, and a guess-the-MVP game.
  • All visualizations: ml-viz

Games & Simulations

  • Fergie Time - Interactive Premier League decision simulator using Monte Carlo simulations to evaluate tactical choices and their impact on expected points.
  • Flappy Bird - Browser-based recreation of the classic Flappy Bird game.
  • Tetris - Browser-based implementation of the classic block-stacking puzzle game.
