Vote share is points won divided by points possible. Players voted through 1979-80 and could not pick teammates; media have voted since 1980-81 on a 10-7-5-3-1 ballot. Earlier ballots varied: some seasons were one vote per ballot (points are then just votes), others used weighted points. Official tallies for 1965-66 to 1968-69, 1970-71 and 1978-79 are incomplete, so those seasons are left out of the closest-race and biggest-blowout records. Stats shown are that season's per-game numbers, and a tie for fifth place shows both players.

Data: Basketball Reference MVP voting pages, 1955-56 through 2025-26.
